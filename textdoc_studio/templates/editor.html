TEXTDOC DIRECTOR

Scene Type Guide

STORY

◉ Narration

Standard documentary scene. Piper narrates while the selected image receives cinematic movement.

§ Chapter

Major section break with large typography. Normally does not use a photograph.

❝ Quote

Prominent quotation with attribution or source.

! Punch

Short dramatic statement. Best at roughly 3–15 words. Uses typography rather than a picture.

Aa Words

Sequential keywords or phrases for rhythmic visual emphasis.

EVIDENCE

▤ Document

Manuscripts, notices, newspaper pages, letters and archival evidence. Preserves document framing.

⌖ Map

Maps and geographic evidence. Designed for location-oriented movement and labels.

VISUAL

▶ B-roll

Visual-first scene with full-frame imagery and cinematic motion underneath narration.
