Welcome to TextDoc Studio, a local-first documentary production system built around story instead of a traditional editing timeline.

The Media Library holds local photographs, documents, maps, and illustrations. The Story Stack represents the production as semantic scenes: narration, chapter, quote, punch, words, document, map, and B-roll.

The Scene Inspector controls narration, visual assignment, labels, and source information. The Scene Guide explains how each treatment behaves. Export preflight checks the project before delivery.

Piper generates narration locally, TextDoc directs the visual treatment, and FFmpeg creates the finished documentary. Completed work can be previewed, downloaded as video or audio, and prepared for a private YouTube upload.

TextDoc Studio. Make the story, not the timeline.
