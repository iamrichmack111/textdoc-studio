TITLE: Witchcraft, Folk Magic, and the Black American Experience

[CHAPTER]
Traditions, Survival, and Change

Across generations, Black communities in the United States have preserved a wide range of spiritual and folk traditions.

[PUNCH]
Their histories are distinct.

[WORDS]
Hoodoo, Rootwork, Vodou, Modern Witchcraft

[QUOTE]
The history is also a story about survival, religion, family knowledge, migration and cultural change.
SOURCE: Documentary narration

These traditions developed in different places and under different historical conditions. Understanding those differences matters.
